# Scene artwork

Flat-colour scenes drawn for Painted Realms on the team's design canvas ("Wandering Storyteller"), November 2026. Original project artwork; no external assets.

- `tale-lantern.svg`, `tale-crown.svg`, `tale-salt.svg`: tale previews (752 × 372, extra sky above the 752 × 300 scene). The `-thumb` files are the list crops; the Hollow Crown thumbnail is framed around the keep.
- `gm-wanderer.svg`, `gm-survivor.svg`, `gm-cursed.svg`: Game Master scenes (860 × 516): forest lantern, arched window, broken arch. The figure is placed by the interface.
- `hero-stage.svg`, `hero-stage-wide.svg`: an arch fading into shadow, a small lantern and a stone plinth (600 × 560). Boots land at y = 485; the wide plinth is for Mira and Ash.

- `region-lanternwood-*.svg`, `region-reach-*.svg`, `region-saltreach-*.svg`: regional scenes (1280 × 720) behind the chapter opening and Ask, from the World page mood boards; `-day` and `-dusk` follow Time of day. Keep landmarks centre and right; the Game Master stands lower left, and on phones the ground about two thirds down meets their feet.

The same scenes are reused wherever a tale appears (setup, journal, invitations), so a place always looks the same.
